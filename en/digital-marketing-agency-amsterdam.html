Frequently asked questions

Can we hire you for just one service?

Yes. Many projects start with just the campaign or just the website. Having the rest in-house doesn’t mean you have to take all of it — it means you don’t have to switch agencies once things grow.

Do you work on a monthly fee or per project?

Both. Campaigns and media management usually run as ongoing monthly support, a website or video production as a fixed-price project. We tell you up front which suits your request.

Who will I be dealing with?

Sten is your fixed point of contact, with Nada alongside him for social advertising. No account manager between you and the people doing the work.

Do you only work in Amstelveen and Amsterdam?

No, we work throughout the Netherlands. Being based here mainly means it’s easy to drop by, and that we know the local market if that’s where you want to grow.
