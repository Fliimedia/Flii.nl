Knowledge base

41 terms from marketing, brand, content, media and data. With a definition, an explanation and a real-world example.

01Marketing

Target audienceThe specific group of people or organisations you want to reach with your marketing and communication. → SegmentationDividing a market into distinct groups with shared characteristics, so you can approach each group in a targeted way. → PositioningThe place your brand occupies in your target audience's mind relative to competitors. → SWOTAn analysis framework covering Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats. → BenchmarkA reference point you measure your own performance against — often based on industry averages or competitors. → FunnelThe path a potential customer takes from first encounter (awareness) to purchase and loyalty. → CTRClick-Through Rate — the percentage of people who click your ad after seeing it. → CPCCost Per Click — what you pay for each click on your ad. → CPLCost Per Lead — the cost of generating one qualified lead. → ROASReturn On Ad Spend — the revenue you generate for every euro you spend on advertising. → ROIReturn On Investment — the return on an investment, expressed as a percentage of the total cost. → ConversionThe moment a visitor takes the action you want: a purchase, sign-up, enquiry or download. → RetargetingAdvertising again to people who visited your website before but have not yet converted. → KPIKey Performance Indicator — a measurable indicator that shows to what extent a goal is being achieved. → Lookalike audiencesAudiences that Meta or Google build based on their similarity to your existing customers. → Affiliate MarketingA performance-based form of marketing in which external partners earn commission by sending traffic or customers to an advertiser through tracking links. → B2C MarketingMarketing aimed at reaching and persuading individual consumers to buy products for personal use. → E-commerceSelling and buying products or services online through websites, apps and digital marketplaces. → BudgetingPlanning, allocating and monitoring financial resources for marketing activities over a set period. → Project managementPlanning, organising, executing and controlling projects to achieve marketing objectives within time, budget and quality parameters. → GovernanceThe structures, policies and responsibilities an organisation uses to manage and account for its marketing activities. → CRMCustomer Relationship Management — the combination of strategy, processes and technology an organisation uses to manage and optimise customer relationships. →

02Brand

Brand identityThe visual and verbal expression of your brand: logo, colour palette, typography, tone of voice and style. → Tone of voiceThe consistent way a brand communicates in writing: the tone, the choice of words and the personality that shines through. → Brand ExperienceThe overall impression a consumer forms across all interactions with a brand — from first contact to post-purchase. → Employer BrandingStrategically positioning an organisation as an attractive employer to attract, engage and retain talent. →

03Content

SEOSearch Engine Optimisation — improving your website so it ranks higher in unpaid search results. → UGCUser Generated Content — content created by users or customers rather than by the brand itself. → Content strategyThe overarching plan that determines how an organisation creates, distributes, manages and measures content to achieve brand and audience goals. → CopyrightThe exclusive right of the creator of an original work to reproduce, distribute and adapt that work, and to give others permission to do so. → AccessibilityDesigning digital products and communication materials so that people with a range of disabilities can use them. →

04Media

Email marketingUsing email as an owned marketing channel to inform, activate and convert subscribers — from one-off campaigns to automated flows. → Display AdvertisingVisual online ads — made up of images, animation or video — shown on websites, apps or platforms outside the search engine. → Mobile MarketingMarketing activities aimed specifically at users on mobile devices, using the mobile context for relevance and reach. → Retail MediaAdvertising space that retailers offer to brands to reach customers at the point of purchase intent, on the retailer's own platform. → Digital MediaOnline media channels and platforms that brands use to reach audiences through internet-connected devices. →

05Data

AttributionAssigning value to the channels and touchpoints that contribute to a conversion. → First-party dataData you collect yourself, directly from your own customers and visitors, without third parties in between. → GDPR / AVGThe European privacy law that regulates the processing of personal data for every organisation that reaches EU residents. → AVG/GDPRThe General Data Protection Regulation (AVG in Dutch) — the European privacy law that protects EU citizens' rights over their personal data. → Privacy & ConsentThe obligation and strategy to collect and process users' personal data in line with privacy law and with explicit consent. →
